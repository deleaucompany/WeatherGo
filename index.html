Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Weather Go respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be used by the application and how that information is handled.

Location Data

Weather Go may access your device location to provide accurate local weather forecasts and weather-related information.

Location data is used solely for app functionality and is not sold to third parties.

Advertising

Weather Go uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect device identifiers and other information required for advertising and analytics services. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

Weather Go offers optional in-app purchases, such as premium features or ad removal. Purchase transactions are securely processed by Apple and/or Google and are not stored by Weather Go.

Local Data Storage

Certain preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device to improve your experience and personalize app behavior.

Third-Party Services

Weather Go may use third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Weather data providers
  • Apple App Store services
  • Google Play services

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Weather Go is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.
